<objective>
Replace the inline `sqlite3` DELETE in `bmtc-retention.service` with a dedicated `retention_cleanup.sh` (D-05, D-06) that runs three ordered deletes in one pass: (1) `ride_segments` past the retention window (existing behavior, now scripted), (2) orphaned `rides` with zero remaining `ride_segments` — the core DATA-03 fix, and (3) `rejection_log` past its 30d TTL — a drive-by fix for a documented-but-unwired cleanup (D-05). The script mirrors `rate_limit_cleanup.sh`'s before/after-count + logging + error-handling style and reads its TTLs from the systemd `EnvironmentFile`.

Purpose: stop `rides` and `rejection_log` from accumulating unbounded rows beyond their retention windows.
Output: `retention_cleanup.sh`, a modified retention service unit, and test coverage seeded with orphan and non-orphan rides.
